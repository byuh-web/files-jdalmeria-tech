Fun Experiences

We had the chance to visit Cantabon cave in Siquijor. It was a fun experience, but it was also a bit scary. The cave was dark and narrow, and we had to crawl through some parts. But it was worth it because we got to see some beautiful rock formations and underground pools. Locals even said that there were foreigners who tried to find gold in the cave, some of them even got lost and never came out.
